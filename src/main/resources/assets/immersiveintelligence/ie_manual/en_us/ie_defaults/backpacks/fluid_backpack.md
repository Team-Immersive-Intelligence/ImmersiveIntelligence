# meta
Canister Backpack
Shake it up
# crafting
|[crafting]{source:"backpack"}|
The Canister Backpack holds up to 16 buckets of one fluid. Hold the canister and use it on a fluid tank to transfer fluid.
# wearing
Wear the canister in the chest slot or the Baubles body slot. To attach it to armor, craft it with a chestplate. Craft the chestplate alone to remove the canister. It keeps its fluid.
