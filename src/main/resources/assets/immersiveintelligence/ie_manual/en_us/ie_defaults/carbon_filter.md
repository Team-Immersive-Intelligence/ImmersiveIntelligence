Carbon Filter
Keepin' it Green!
# intro
|[crafting]{source:"co2_filter"}|
Carbon dioxide, an oft-unwanted byproduct of combustion, finds new use in the [rubber production](rubber_production.md#chemical_steps) process.
# operation
The filter is placed on the sides or top of the topmost block of an [Improved Blast Furnace](improvedBlastfurnace) to collect the carbon during the steelmaking process.
Connect a pipe to the Filter to route the carbon dioxide elsewhere.
