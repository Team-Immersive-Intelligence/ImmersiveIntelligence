# meta
Advanced Capacitor Backpack
(Almost) Infinite Power
# powerpack_1
|[crafting]{source:"backpack"}|
Advanced Capacitor Backpack is a direct upgrade to the [Capacitor Backpack](powerpack), with the main difference being more energy capacity and an ability to be charged by [Tesla Coils](emplacement_weapons.md#tesla0) or by [standing inside uninsulated wiring.]
# powerpack_2
Its wireless charging ability also makes the wearer [immune to electric damage] and removes the need of wearing the Faraday Suit. The Backpack's side sacks can be painted the same way as leather armor.
# wearing
The backpack can be worn in the chest slot or the Baubles body slot. Craft it with a chestplate to attach it to armor. Craft the chestplate alone to remove it. The capacitor keeps its charge and paint colour.
