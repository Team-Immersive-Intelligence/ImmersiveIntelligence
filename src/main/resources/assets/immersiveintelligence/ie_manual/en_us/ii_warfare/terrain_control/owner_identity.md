# intro
Every chunk, multiblock machine, vehicle, and certain other structures are [Properties](ii_warfare/terrain_control/chunks_and_properties.md), and can be owned.
Ownership is represented by an **Owner Identity** - often simply called a **Faction**.
By default, everyone is *the owner of their personal identity*, but it is possible to join another faction, to share ownership.
