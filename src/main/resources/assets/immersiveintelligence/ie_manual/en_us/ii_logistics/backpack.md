Backpack

# crafting
|[crafting]{source:"backpack"}|
The Backpack has 18 storage slots. Hold it and right-click to open it. Sneak and right-click to put it on.
# wearing
Wear a backpack in the chest slot or the Baubles body slot. To wear it over armor, craft it with a chestplate. Put the chestplate alone in the crafting grid to recover the backpack and its contents.
